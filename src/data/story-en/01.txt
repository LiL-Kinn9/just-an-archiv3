From the sky, one was cast out of paradise.
He fell aimlessly toward the mortal world, his magnificent wings slowly fading away.
All that remained was black, a color deeper than the very self of mankind, staining his wings.
Every word a lament against fate.
He said:
" Someday, without fail. "
" I will drag all of heaven down to the very bottom of the earth. "
The smile on his lips lasted only a few seconds, and he simply went on leaving the sky behind.
Then he fell unconscious.
