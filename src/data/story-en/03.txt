" Tiffany Kristen. "
" If you like, call me Kristin. "
He mumbled each broken syllable inside his mouth, unaware that one who had once ruled the heavens was stumbling over a human name.
" K..Ris.. "
" It's Kris..Tin. "
She spoke softly, breaking his hesitation. Her slender brows knitted slightly, unwittingly sinking his awkwardness even deeper.
" Yes, I know. You need not repeat it like that. "
" And you? What should I call you? "
A name? It had been a very long time since anyone dared to speak his name, and he himself had long left it to sink into oblivion. But the woman before him was still waiting. His gaze swept over every roof tile, every crooked sign of the market, trying to snatch any word from among the crude lettering, but in vain.
Then, behind Kristin, in a hidden corner, a rotting notice board caught his eye. Pressed into the decaying wood were two portraits along with a few torn lines of words and numbers. The first frame sketched a long, slender creature covered in pallid white scales, a name carved beneath it.
DRAGON.
The drawing beside it had been more than half torn away, leaving only a black patch of nothingness.
DÉMON.
In the midst of that darkness, a single blazing eye remained, staring straight at him.
Kristin rose onto her toes, using her small frame to block his view, her lashes lifting in scrutiny.
" What are you looking at? "
" Nothing.. I was just... "
The cover-up crumbled in his throat, only making this conversational trap more suffocating.
" Dragalon Démonic. "
" What? Your name is Dragalon? "
He gritted his teeth and nodded, not yet brave enough to face that searching gaze.
" Yes. "
" And Démonic? "
" Yes.. "
Kristin made to turn around, curious to see what behind her had been holding his attention all this time. He immediately stepped in, leaning over to shield it like a thief afraid of being exposed. She tilted her shoulder to the left; he shifted to block her. His stubbornness finally forced her to give up.
" So where do you come from? "
He began to find human curiosity truly bothersome. Or, deep down, he simply did not want to stir up the sacred place that had cast him out.
" I come from the south of the continent. "
" So you're Dragalon Démonic from the south? "
He turned his blazing red eyes toward a lifeless corner of the street, letting her muttering keep striking his ears.
" Dragalon.. Démonic.. Who named you? It sounds so weird. "
His brows knitted tight. This woman had the nerve to mock the name he had just painstakingly scraped together.
" I think I need to eat something. And you? Do you want to eat with me? "
Before he could even open his mouth, slender fingers had closed around his wrist and were dragging him away.
" I think asking you is the biggest waste of time I've ever done in my life. "
He lowered his eyes to the small hand clinging to him. A strangely soft sensation, more pleasant even than the coat she had bought him. He told himself it was only because this body was not yet used to human warmth.
Despite his proud ego howling in protest, he simply walked obediently beside her, not bothering to ask where they were going, not caring about the filthy scenery around them. His blazing red eyes rested on only one thing: that small hand.
" We're here, I usually eat here. I think you should try a few dishes too. "
She hauled him through the battered wooden door of the Ditovias tavern. The smell of charred roast lamb struck his nose. The room was packed with drunkards, each one clutching a mug of beer overflowing with foam. He loathed the crowding, and the rank smell of human flesh never stopped making him sick.
Kristin cleared the way, leading him to one of the few empty tables in a corner.
" Wait for me a bit, I'll order food for us. "
He sat down imposingly. Before he could even look around, he noticed the scrutinizing glances aimed at him, bringing waves of whispers that grew louder and louder. The crowd seemed to sniff out an aura that did not belong to their world. Just as the air was about to break, Kristin calmly sat down across from him, two hot plates of food in hand, and pushed the smoky plate toward him.
" Roast lamb, the specialty here. Someone like you probably only eats meat. "
He stared at the charred carcass of the unlucky beast. The name mankind used for this city suddenly rang in his head: The City of Lambs. He glanced at her modest portion.
" What is that? "
" Roasted broccoli. Why? Do you want to try it? "
He frowned and shook his head, reluctantly turning his attention back to the slab of lamb oozing fat.
" I have to eat this, do I? "
Kristin's eyelids twitched in helplessness.
" Nope. You don't have to eat it. Give it to the guys around us. "
His arrogance did not let him notice the sarcasm. He silently stood up, holding the plate with both hands, about to turn away.
" Can you sit down and just eat it? I don't have much money, you know! "
He stared blankly at her grumbling face, then sullenly dropped the plate back onto the table.
" You humans are truly hard to understand. "
" You're the one who's hard to understand! Eat. You're such a fuss. "
He tore off a piece of meat with his bare hands, threw it into his mouth and chewed reluctantly. Then another bite. This mortal flavor turned out not as dreadful as he had scorned it to be. At some point Kristin had propped her chin on her hand, her eyes quietly tracing every line of the face across from her.
" So why weren't you wearing anything when you met me? "
His teeth stopped mid-bite. The piece of meat he had just torn off quietly went back onto the plate.
" Actually... in the south, we look like that. "
" Like what? "
She leaned forward, her pupils wide as if to swallow every evasion. This woman truly knew how to drive one who had once ruled the clouds into a corner.
" We are.. Wild. "
Kristin held her position, waiting.
" And... reckless. "
She could not hold back and burst out laughing.
" What does that even meaan? "
He frowned, not understanding at all what was so laughable in his lie. But deep in his cold heart, he felt relieved watching that smile. He told himself it was only curiosity about humans.
" So what does someone wild and reckless like you come to the north for? Are you looking for something? "
His throat tightened as if lodged with an invisible lump of lead, though this question was hardly as tricky as the ones she had asked before.
Looking for what?
The question led his gaze toward the shabby window, where the last feeble light of the dying day was being swallowed by the night. He could not count how many such cycles had passed since this body fell from the sky. He had only drifted on heavy feet, without direction, without purpose. Her question had dragged that lonely chaos into the light, and he only wanted to bury it forever in the abyss.
She tapped her fingers on the table.
" Hey. "
He slowly moved his eyes back to her, heavily, as if facing his own ruined self. But Kristin did not demand an answer. She only quietly pushed her broccoli toward him.
" Or eat it for me. I'm a bit full. Don't think too much. Just eat first. "
He stared at her in a daze for a moment, then at the lifeless sprigs of broccoli. Moments ago he had scorned this bland food, but the longer he looked, the more strangely alluring it became. He picked a small sprig and put it in his mouth. His tongue tasted no sweetness, yet a strange flavor soothed his throat in a way he could not name. He simply went on eating in silence, under her unwavering gaze.
Until the tavern door was kicked open.
The whole room instantly fell silent. A fat man walked in, each step pounding the wooden floor as though the whole city lay beneath his boots. He wore a coarse bearskin cloak, and on his chest was a badge engraved with an eye, encased in dazzling gold. Drag's eyes fixed on that treacherous metal. In some twisted corner of his mind, he had seen this symbol before.
Behind the nobleman came henchmen with fierce faces, hands gripping swords not yet drawn from their sheaths. The crowd immediately bowed their heads. No one dared to meet their eyes, except him.
" All of you, hear these words clearly! "
He flung a heavy cloth sack onto a table. The coins inside clashed with a piercing clink.
" We're looking for a monster that appeared at the border a few days ago. "
Drag shrugged indifferently and kept chewing the remaining broccoli.
" It has the shape of a man. "
Kristin turned around unconsciously. The fork in her hand slowly came down onto the wood.
" It has blazing red eyes. "
Drag's attention was drawn again by the strange utensil she had just put down. Curious, he reached out to take it, but she hastily flicked the fork away and lunged forward to grip his hand. Her hands were ice cold.
" With a pair of wings.. "
She pulled hard, as if to force him out of his seat that very instant.
" As black as ashes. "
The nobleman tossed the sack of money into the middle of the room.
" Anyone with any clue at all... "
Drag looked up at her panic, completely lost.
" Huh? "
" Let's get out of here, " she whispered, very fast. " Keep your head down. Don't look at anyone. "
" But I haven't even... "
" Hurry! "
Her grip tightened as if pressing into his flesh. He obediently stood up, his eyes looking back regretfully at the unfinished plate. A cold glint flashed past. A blade was already at his throat the moment they straightened up.
" Hold on. "
One of the henchmen narrowed his eyes and glared at the tall stranger.
" I've never seen you around here. "
Drag's gaze passed over the sword, as flimsy as a dry twig, then turned dully to the fool holding it.
" I have not seen you either. But why is your hand shaking? "
" I'm not.. Why would I shake before someone harmless like you. "
Drag calmly reached out with his bare hand and grasped the sharpest part of the blade. His fingers tightened one by one. A shattering sound rang out, and the whole tavern held its breath.
" It's you! The monster. "
He was already too used to the names mankind bestowed upon him. The nobleman stepped back, stunned that the prey he had put a price on was standing tall right before him.
" Cut off its wings! "
Two henchmen charged, swords whipped from their sheaths. But Kristin was faster. Before the steel could even be raised, she used her meager strength to yank his shoulder down, as if she knew in advance that something terrible was about to happen. The two blades ripped through the empty air where his neck had just been. With perfectly controlled strength, he flung her into a safe corner. The two swords immediately changed direction, crashing down toward his skull.
He did not dodge.
His bare hands rose to catch the steel coming down. The metal cut deep into his flesh, dark red liquid gushing from the gashes at his wrists. Then suddenly, the flow stopped dead.
" Still too weak. "
From the corner of his eye, he glimpsed her slumped in the corner where he had flung her. Her face was deathly pale. She was not looking at the swords, nor at the blood. She was looking straight at his wrist, where the red had just stopped flowing, and she had heard those words. He had no time to read that expression.
With a weightless twist, he snapped the blade and grabbed a shard like a dagger. In the blink of an eye, no creature in the room could follow his movement. Two heavy lumps of flesh slumped to the floor. Not a scream, not a single last word. Those throats had been shredded at some point unseen.
The drunkards shoved one another to flee through the door. Chairs toppled, beer mugs shattered on the floor. Drag walked coldly up to the nobleman, whose fleshy face had now been drained of all color.
" A moment ago, you seemed to be the loudest of them. "
The man backed away until his back hit the wall. His legs went limp, but Drag gave him no chance to kneel. The man's eyes twitched again and again before the red burning at the bottom of the beast's eyes, which carried only one message.
-Death-
His fat body was lifted into the air as if every law of mother earth had become meaningless. His throat was squeezed tight, every breath broken.
" I hear you were looking for me? So what do you want now? "
Moments ago, he had believed himself the hunter in control. Now, dangling from the devil's hand like a sickly rabbit, he finally understood that he was not even worthy of being prey.
" Please, I'll give you everything.. You want money, I'll give you money. Let me live. "
Drag squeezed harder.
" Live? You are hardly worthy of it. Those more arrogant than I have never survived. Ah, by the way, how much is in the sack you brought? "
His throat was parched, every pitiful syllable choking in it.
" Fo..ur.. Hun..dred Chromee. "
Drag lowered his eyes to his own soiled coat. Four Chromee for a set of clothes. Four hundred for his head.
" So that is not much at all? "
" Please.. Le.. "
He let go. The heavy body fell to the floor, and the blind joy of surviving masked the pain he should have felt from the very first second. Blood gushed from his throat, draining the strength from his panicked legs. He lurched onto the sticky wooden table, the one where he had so forcefully flung the sack of money moments ago. All his glory and arrogance collapsed along with his severed throat. The rank red slowly mixed into the sour mug of beer on the table.
The Ditovias tavern sank into silence. Only he remained.
He opened the cloth sack. The Chromee glittered under the oil lamp. He picked out four coins, clenched them in his palm, and left the rest to soak in the pool of blood. Four pieces of silver. No more.
He slowly walked back to the table where, moments ago, a little warmth still lingered. The corner where she had slumped was empty too. That woman had vanished into nothingness at some point.
He unconsciously picked up the half-bitten sprig of broccoli and put it into his bloodstained mouth. It was bland and tasteless. Not because the food had gone cold, but because the seasoning of eating with her was gone. His eyes landed on the iron fork she had flicked away in panic. Now he could pick it up at his leisure, but the desire suddenly went cold when the one forbidding him was no longer there.
-Are you looking for something?-
An echo from a close past suddenly rushed back, carrying a weight that crushed his lungs.
" Kristin? "
For the first time, the name slipped from his lips in full.
" ..Kristin. "
