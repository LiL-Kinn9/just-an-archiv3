A girl ran as fast as she could toward the Endless Dusk mountains, where the distant peaks had all but merged into the dark red clouds covering the horizon. She crossed the forest that had once clothed the foot of the mountains in green, now nothing but the remnants of eternal fires. The trees had burned into lumps of charcoal, crowding together beneath the blazing sky. On those parched branches hung people.
Or at least, what was left of them.
There was a man hung across a trunk, his belly barely whole, long lengths of entrails swirling in the wind like torn ribbons, glimpsed and lost again behind the ash drifting across the forest. On another tree, a woman was impaled through the middle of her chest, her bleeding fingers still clawing at the bark. Meaningless efforts against a favor granted by heaven. The Miracle still kept its pure promise, only it had never promised that eternal life would be a blessing.
Two roars echoed from afar. Bel'vi looked back. Two Darksiders were charging through the tangled thornbushes, tearing at each other as they ran, as if even at the end of the world they still had to fight over who had the right to kill a girl first.
" How annoying. "
She leapt onto a giant root clinging to the cliff face, both hands gripping the rough bark, and climbed. A claw stabbed down right beside her foot. Missed. The second time, a pitch-black hand seized her ankle.
" Damn it. "
She lost her balance and dangled from the edge of the root, one hand clinging to a vine. The monster raised its head, mouth gaping, its long teeth crowded together like a bear trap. She looked down, then raised her other hand to her ear and pulled the silver hand away from The Ear. Every sound came crashing in, and she gritted her teeth and bore it. The metal softened, flowed between her fingers, stretched, curved into a knife with a serpentine blade. Ancient script appeared along the blade.
The Whispers of Negated Pains.
" Get your hand off me. "
She threw it straight down. The knife lodged in the middle of the creature's forehead, and the beast's hand let go at once. But that was only the beginning. Lines of black script crawled off the blade, slithered down its face, then spread across its whole body. The masses of flesh beneath its skin began to move.
A scream escaped.
Then another.
Then hundreds. Thousands. Tens of thousands. Voices that did not belong to the monster. They poured out of every crack in its body: the sounds she heard every day and every night, when there was no silver to protect her. The Darksider convulsed violently.
BOOM.
Its body exploded. Blood and flesh splattered across the root, then slowly slid down into the deep grooves of the wood. The Whispers sprang free of the shattered head, fell, and melted the moment it touched the ground.
She had barely looked up when the second one arrived. It gripped the trunk with all four limbs, drew its body back taut, then launched itself up hard. She only had time to open her eyes wide. A thin red line swept across, so light it almost did not exist. The Darksider kept lunging a few more steps, as if even it had not understood what had just happened. Then its body split in two.
The two halves fell, still moving.
" That is the second time, Bel. "
She turned around. Drag was hovering behind her, the red sword in his hand dissolving back into blood. She glared at him resentfully.
" None of your business, who asked you to save me? "
He did not answer. He flew over, wrapped an arm around her and lifted her off the root. Her cheeks puffed out, her arms crossed over her chest. He glanced at her.
" You are ridiculous. "
" Huh? "
" I cannot believe I have met someone as whiny as you. "
" You— "
Before she could finish, they both fell silent.
Before them, a colossal mass of matter stood towering, blotting out the whole stretch of sky behind it. It pierced through the clouds, linking the lower world with The City of Mercy Dreams floating in the red sky. The golden hand that had appeared when the Miracle began no longer kept its original shape. Its five fingers had stretched down into the earth, driving deep through the mountainside and the layers of stone below, becoming the giant roots she had just clung to, winding across the ground. The palm had swollen wide, stretching into a single trunk that pierced straight through the clouds and pinned itself to the bottom of the city above.
He looked up. If twenty of Manlanier's towers were set side by side, they still would not cover the breadth of that trunk.
" Do you remember the tree where the ancestors of mankind made their greatest mistake? "
" What tree? "
" You don't know Adam and Eve carried a grave sin for eating the forbidden fruit from that tree? "
" No. "
" Are you serious? "
" Does my face look like I am joking? "
She sighed.
" People call it The Root of Sin. The ancestors of mankind ate the forbidden fruit from it. Their punishment was to have their bodies bound in its roots, as a symbol of faith. A warning so their descendants would forever remember that mankind is born already carrying a grave sin. "
He looked at the roots driven through the mountain. An old, unpleasant feeling came back. It was not quite a memory, he could not even recall anything, but his body seemed to know that thing. Like when he stood before the colossal statue in Mother of Mothers, like standing before something he had once knelt beneath. Or had once wanted to destroy.
" But people cut it down a very long time ago. Right here in these very mountains. They didn't need gods anymore. Or at least they once thought so. "
He looked up at the city in the sky. A tree that had died, a symbol that had been torn down, and now The High Wills had raised it again in the middle of the end of the world. Without her explaining any further, he understood. The Root of Sin had not returned out of mercy. It was a sacrament made flesh, a warning, a forgetting rekindled.
But neither of them had time to admire the beauty of the divine.
Whoosh.
The ear-splitting rush of wind reached The Ear. She whipped around. A golden sword was hurtling straight down from above. She kicked Drag in the chest.
CRASH.
The two of them were flung apart. The sword pierced the root right where he had just been standing, cracks running out around the blade. He looked down. White carnations were beginning to bloom.
He did not need to look at the one who wielded it. He already knew.
" Kristin. "
She tilted her head.
" Seriously? "
---
A white figure appeared beside the sword, so fast it was as if she had always been standing there. Eleven white wings were folded behind her. He looked at her, longer than necessary, as if searching for something. A glance. A moment of hesitation. Any sign proving that the one standing before him was still the girl he had once known.
She gave him nothing at all. She grasped the hilt and pulled it free, a golden line sweeping across. He pulled Bel'vi against himself before that razor-sharp thing could touch her, and both of them fell off the root. One hand held her, the other formed a sign.
BOOM.
A sphere of blood shot past Kristin's shoulder and slammed into the trunk behind her. Splinters of wood and red dust burst out, swept by the high winds into a thin veil between them. He had barely spread his wings when a golden light tore through the dust.
Thunk.
The sword pierced his chest. Blood splattered onto Kristin's face; she had already appeared right beside the hilt.
" Seems I still haven't hit your heart, huh? "
Words so light they were almost familiar. It was only a pity that between them now lay two opposing sides. He looked at her left eye. A silver hand, much like the one Bel'vi wore on her ear, was covering The Eye completely. She gripped the hilt and planted both feet on his stomach. Buds began to show around the blade, and her eleven wings burst open hard.
CRASH.
He was flung toward the forest, a sharp branch piercing straight through his stomach. He looked to the side. Beside him, a man was also pinned on a branch, still breathing, still looking at him. The man moved his cracked lips.
" At least it is only your first time. "
He snapped the branch, pulled his body free of it, the strands of flesh closing at once. He did not look at the man again, and flew away from that place, his eyes sweeping around. Kristin. Bel'vi. Rather troublesome, he was not sure whom he should look for first. Finally, in the distance, he saw Bel'vi hanging upside down from a branch. He dove down and caught her.
" You took forever, I'm about to get vertigo. "
He sighed. A little stupid normalcy, in the middle of a day where nothing was normal anymore. He carried her back up.
" You need to keep your distance from me. She is only attacking me. "
She looked at him.
" A personal grudge? "
He did not answer. In the past, he would have told her to shut her mouth. This time he only said one more thing.
" Do not hurt her. "
She turned to him. He was trying to find a figure.
" There'll be a way. "
She fell silent, then stepped back. A very small sound reached The Ear.
" DODGE! "
The golden sword flew at him like a spear. He summoned a red blade from his blood and raised it before his chest. Just before the two blades met, Kristin appeared. She twisted her wrist, the sword changed course, and he turned with it.
CLANG.
The two blades ground against each other, sparks of red and gold bursting from the point of impact. The distance between them was so close he could see clearly every streak of dried blood on her face. A distance that had once meant something entirely different.
" You know full well I cannot die. So why do you still try to kill me? "
She gripped the hilt.
" If you're the reason for the end of the world, then I'll be the one who has to stop you. "
His eyes softened, as if a current of thought had just touched his heart. He understood. She had never stood before him because she wanted to be his enemy. She only could not turn her back on the people below. Just as he had never truly wanted to fight her; he was only trying to pull her out of the fate that had bound her. Both his hands loosened.
The golden sword slid past his guard. Cut across his neck. Blood ran down.
She froze, her eyes wide.
" Why did you do that? Doesn't it hurt? "
The question steadied the thought he had just had.
" You have run me through how many times now, and you ask that? "
She fell silent. Her eyes slid away from him, just for a moment. Then she spoke, very quietly.
" I never wanted Dragalon to be Lucifer. "
The sword still rested on his neck, but he forgot it. Some wounds are truly troublesome; they do not need to pierce skin or flesh.
" What? "
She said nothing more, only quietly avoided his eyes. The blade pressed deeper, then pulled away. Blood ran down his shoulder. He still stood motionless, the wound not closing. One second. Then two. As if his very body were resisting the curse running through his veins. In the end, the curse went on. Flesh crawled together, skin closed.
She hurled the sword up high; it lodged in a great branch of The Root of Sin, and she instantly appeared beside it. He still stood below. At some point, a drop of water had fallen onto his face. He knew what it was. Not rain, nor blood.
Blood from the cut on his neck had already dripped onto the ground beside his feet, into a small red stain. He did not bother to look. Bel'vi stood a few steps away, right at the foot of the tree.
" Are you okay? "
He did not answer. She fell silent too. Among the tens of thousands of shrill sounds out there, two words seemed to exist in silence within The Ear, and she was the one who heard them most clearly.
-Why?-
Those two words were like a drop of water falling onto a still lake. She could not describe their shape.
His ash-grey wings spread wide. He flew up, her voice still behind him, and he did not stop. There was something in him that still refused to disappear. Not the belief that Kristin was wrong; he no longer even knew who was right. Only a very foolish belief that somewhere behind The Eye, behind Krisanta, behind everything she was trying to become, Kristin was still there.
He gripped the hilt. Black fire flared up. He threw it.
The sword shot straight toward Kristin. She did not dodge, and The Eye did not glow either. The blade grazed her shoulder and plunged deep into The Root of Sin. She turned around. Something was still on the hilt. A hand, still gripping tight, its fingers nearly splitting, a length of bone beginning to grow from the wrist. She immediately pulled out her sword and stabbed into the bone taking shape. The golden blade caught between vertebrae, strands of muscle twining around it, and a ribcage appeared. No breath, only bone and flesh that seemed to be rotting.
And in the midst of all of it, a heart of gold that never stopped stirring.
Thump-thump.
She looked at that heart. Something made her freeze for a beat. Blood vessels clung to the fragments of bone, then crawled toward the trapped sword. Every strand of his flesh strangled the blade, and the blade began to crack. She stepped closer and gently laid her hand on the heart.
At that very spot, he remembered this same hand, back when it was still warm, caressing his chest in the church of Mother of Mothers.
White carnations grew from where she touched. Their roots pierced through the golden vessels, crept between her fingers, threads of blood winding around every petal. The heart contracted hard. The other organs of his body slowed in their healing. Her face was close to his. Only one side of his face was whole, only one eye, but that was enough to see the pupil settle in her eye. It was regret, very small, very faint, wavering as if even she did not want to do this. He did not understand. His mind was empty, and his healing stopped completely. Between them lay knots that had never fallen from their lips, but time was a cruel thing that had never allowed them the chance to confide.
A premonition rose within her. When she touched his heart, one of his arms had not yet finished regrowing. She looked down. The other arm had taken shape, strands of muscle wrapped around two fingers pointing downward.
BOOM.
The branch beneath their feet exploded. She lost her balance, her eleven wings opening on reflex. She fell first, he fell a beat after, and at the very bottom, at the foot of the tree, Bel'vi looked up. His body finished forming its missing parts, then shot straight toward her. She turned, bracing for the worst.
Only a tight embrace.
She went rigid. The silver hand over her left eye melted, and The Eye appeared before him, a pale gold. Its pupil darted back and forth, as if searching for some reasonable thing to warn of, and caught nothing. She still waited. No sword appeared, no finger was raised. Only two arms holding her tight, while both of them were still falling.
---
Her warmth carried memories flowing back into his mind: a few laughs behind his back, a few old stories she told all along the church corridors. He remembered two arms that had once wrapped around his neck. She was so light that he only wanted to walk a few beats slower, just to make the road longer. And of course, he had never admitted it. Thinking back now, there were quite a lot of things he had never admitted in front of her. Troublesome, really.
And there were also many things he had never admitted to himself.
Drag opened his eyes and looked down. Bel'vi was staring open-mouthed at the two of them. A current of thought passed through his mind, without a single word spoken aloud.
-Throw it, Bel-
Her eyes went round. She heard it.
" You've really lost your mind, Lucifer! "
The silver hand flowed down from her ear, and The Whispers of Negated Pains appeared in her hand. She aimed at Kristin.
" I'm sorry, sis. It's because of him... "
The dagger shot toward Kristin, the ancient script on its blade blazing. Kristin turned her face, and The Eye lit up at once. She grabbed Drag's shoulder and twisted hard, the two of them switching places in an instant, putting him between her and the blade flying up from below. She believed she was still in control. A reasonable choice. If its course did not change, the blade would plunge straight into his back, not hers. The Eye had never been wrong, and he knew its power all too well.
And precisely because he knew, he had waited for her to do it.
His two fingers pointed down, at the small stain of blood at the foot of the tree, the blood that had flowed from her own cut. The body holding Kristin began to dissolve, his arms going soft, his skin turning into streaks of blood that clung tight to her wings. They dried almost instantly, holding all eleven wings fast, not giving her a single chance to swing them. Below, the stain of blood rose into the air, forming a golden heart within a skeleton, then flesh wrapped around it layer by layer, and his body took shape somewhere else. Only now did she understand clearly. He did not need a hand, did not need a head. As long as a single part of his body remained, it would still be him.
Even if only a single drop of blood remained.
Thunk. Light as a feather. Bel'vi's dagger lodged in her shoulder, too late for her to realize it. The ancient lines of script crept through her skin like a stream of poison.
Drag did not think. He spread his wings, needing only to catch her before that body hit the ground. But before his hands could reach her, they suddenly stopped. From high above, a booming voice crashed into his head.
" Lucifer. Tear off that filthy thing! "
Suddenly his arms lifted behind his back. He looked around; nothing was lifting them. His fingers seized his own shoulders. He tried to let go, but could not. A feeling of revulsion ran down his body. For the first time, even this body belonged to someone else.
" No— "
RIP.
His hands yanked hard. His ash-grey wings were torn from his back, and he himself was the one who did it. Blood sprayed everywhere, ash-grey feathers scattering across the whole sky. Pain ran through his whole body, but did not linger long.
Kristin was still falling.
And so was he.
He looked up. Far, far above, behind the clouds, a figure flickered, too far away to make out its face. The voice had crashed down from there. But Drag no longer cared who it was. His eyes followed only one person.
Kristin.
Her body was falling. And this time, he had nothing left to catch her with.
---
Kristin's eleven wings had broken free of the dried blood, but her body no longer had the strength to open them. Bel'vi ran to where she was about to fall and reached out both arms to catch her. The force of the impact knocked them both onto a low root, Bel'vi's feet skidding along the bark.
She pulled the knife from Kristin's shoulder, then pressed her palm to the wound. The lines of ancient script crawling over Kristin's body flowed back one by one into her palm, as if being gathered in. Kristin's brows knitted.
" Are you okay, sis? I'm sorry, he made me do it... "
Kristin slowly opened her eyes and looked at her. The lines of script had left her skin, but in Kristin's eyes there were still thousands of somethings screaming. Kristin gripped the girl's hand tight.
" Why...? Why do all of them wish to die? "
Bel'vi did not answer. She rubbed Kristin's shoulder. Since the Miracle appeared, she had never been able to answer that question herself either. But there was something she kept to herself, unspoken. Not the pain of the thousands of souls below, but the dull, aching pain of the woman before her, like something caught deep beneath the surface of a still lake, struggling yet never able to reach the surface.
Kristin turned her head to look toward Drag. Bel'vi suddenly caught her hand before she could stand. The Ear had heard something.
---
Only when he saw Bel'vi catch Kristin did he realize his breath had just stopped. Not far away, among the clouds shredded by the battle, both his shoulders split open. From there, a length of bone stretched out longer than half his body, bundles of muscle wrapped around it, and ash-grey feathers covered it. His wings had returned.
He looked up at The City of Mercy Dreams hanging above The Root of Sin. At the highest point of the city, a figure sat motionless among the stone towers and golden light, as if the chaos below did not trouble him at all. Drag headed straight toward the city, the sword lengthening in his palm.
A very small thought slipped into his mind, so small he tried to pretend it had never appeared. He had never imagined things would turn out like this. The people below. The Root of Sin. And her too. Perhaps it truly was all because of him. Only because he still existed, everything he wanted to hold on to was, in the end, shattering one by one.
He closed his eyes for a moment, his chest swelling. The sword of the apocalypse blazed in his palm. He swung it through the air. The blade tore through the wind, forming a black streak that shot straight toward the figure atop the city. The slash split the clouds in two, dragging a long stretch of darkness across the blazing red sky. Nothing in its path was strong enough to stop it.
Or it ought to stop.
His heart throbbed for one beat. Behind that blade, a figure as light as mist. Familiar.
" Kristin? "
He still tried to deny the name that had just fallen from his mouth. But the golden light behind the clouds only confirmed his thought. The slash rushed closer. The clouds were cut to either side, revealing eleven white wings folding closed amid a sky full of ash and dust.
" No. "
The Eye would tell her. He truly believed so. Surely so. But the figure of that woman still did not waver, not even a little.
-Why?-
The two words passed through his head so fast that his throat went dry and raw. She still hovered before him, looking at him the way she had when they ate at Ditovias. Back then, he had been annoyed by Kristin asking foolish questions, and only wanted to avoid them. But now he could not take his eyes off her.
Perhaps it truly ought to stop.
A single tear slowly rolled down her cheek, only from her right eye. Surely it was the same drop that had fallen onto his face earlier. The corners of her lips curved, a very brief smile. He still did not understand what that feeling was. The Eye shone brighter than before, the last time it would warn Kristin of danger. But nothing changed except that light.
The slash passed straight through her body.
Not a single sound told him. Not even his heart, which had stopped at some point without his knowing.
Amid the freshly torn clouds, her body lost its footing and fell. He immediately shot toward her, seeming to forget even that he could fly. Only a little more. He reached out and caught her waist, pulling her tight into his arms. The one sitting high above was no longer something he cared about. His two wings wrapped around, enfolding her, and he plunged through the small branches growing thick from The Root of Sin. Splinters of wood cut deep into him and lodged in his back. That pain mattered nothing compared to the woman he was shielding.
They kept falling along the giant trunk, through ash and dust and broken wood, until the base of The Root of Sin appeared below. His wings opened slightly. She still lay curled in his arms, lighter than he remembered. Or perhaps his heavy thoughts made him believe so.
He hurriedly set her down against the trunk. A red stain was spreading across her white dress. He tore a small strip of cloth from her clothes and pressed it to the hole in her chest. Things were worse than he had thought. If only he had never thrown that slash of hatred. Blood crept between his fingers.
" Kristin? "
No response. He tore another strip of cloth and pressed it in.
" Kristin!? "
A faint cough escaped. Just that was enough to pull his heartbeat back. He leaned closer.
" Can you hear me? "
Her eyes slowly opened, gently lifting to look at him. Her breathing was weak; he felt it through every rise and fall growing smaller beneath his palm. The splinters in his back fell away one by one, skin and flesh knitting back. It was only a pity that such a thing only happened to one like him. The words he spoke seemed to choke at his tongue.
" It is all right, I will stay here with you. I will find a way... "
Another foolish lie. But at least, this time it was no longer for the sake of his arrogant nature.
He tore off more strips of cloth, his hands taking turns covering the gap in her chest, only hoping they could slow her blood a little longer. Just a little longer. Suddenly a gentle warmth touched his cheek. He no longer cared about anything happening around them.
" It's okay. "
Fingers caressed his face.
" It's okay, really. "
His lips pressed tight. He hated that voice. He hated her blood clinging to his hands, because he knew this had happened because of him, and because the ground around her was slowly soaking in that blood too. His body was trembling abnormally; he had never known it could do that. He could no longer even master his own body.
" BEL'VI!! "
He roared.
" Someone, anyone at all. Please... help her. "
He had never feared anything before, not even when his body was torn apart, not even death, a thing he had never met. Now that fear appeared, it was so great that none of his fingers were steady anymore, so great that an arrogant one like him had to beg for a mercy.
Her fingers moved down and rested on his lips.
" Shhhh. You're too loud... It's okay, really. "
He kept struggling to wipe away the blood running down both her sides, his hands never stopping their shaking.
" Listen to me. "
She coughed, blood running from the corner of her lips. He took her hand and gripped it tight, as if he did not want the nightmare to happen even once more.
" I think... I had to do it. "
" Why? Why would you do this?! That eye, does it not tell you everything?! "
He still held her hand. He thought that if he let go, the woman before him would leave once again.
" You saw it, it was coming. I know you saw it! So why? "
Her thumb gently stroked each of his knuckles. The trembling inside him stopped.
" I saw... more of you. "
He did not understand those words, and did not want himself to understand.
" No... No, Kristin. I will keep you alive. "
He fooled himself once more. And she knew it full well. Her gentle eyes understood every word he said. In the middle of the end of the world, no one could save them.
No miracle would appear.
" I always thought... "
" My instincts were never wrong. "
" And it's true... they never were. "
Her eyes grew heavy.
" One half stays... One half leaves. "
A gentle smile appeared on her face.
" I tried to make one half of it leave. I tried to destroy it so many times... "
He reached up and wiped the red from the corner of her lips.
" But you know what?... "
She lowered her hand to rest on his chest.
" I realized... nothing could ever make it stop. "
He bit his lip hard. Her voice grew heavier and heavier, so heavy his chest felt pulled taut by thousands of chains.
" This Mark chose me... But this time... I'm the one who chooses. "
Her hand grew colder and colder, cold as the faint mist in a dream.
" BEL'VI!! HELP ME! ...please... "
The bloodstains spread across the ground. Between the cracks of the roots, carnations kept blooming around the place where the two of them sat.
" You're always like this, aren't you?... So stubborn... "
He knew she was not blaming him now. But he wanted her to blame him, wanted her to frown when he did something she did not like. Wanted another meal. Another autumn. Somewhere they had more time. It was only a pity he knew he would never escape this eternal nightmare.
Her breathing grew fainter.
" I want... you to do something for me. "
" Anything... as long as you stay. "
The flowers grew thicker around them, swaying gently in the last breaths of wind beneath the canopy of roots. He leaned slightly closer.
" I want... you to love yourself... the way you love me. "
The hand on his chest gently fell. He hurried to catch it.
" Kristin? "
He waited for a response. The light of The Eye slowly faded, as if the Mark were allowing her to become human one last time. No more color of loss. Only a pupil clouded over, reflecting his image clearly. Only him, forever. Her eyelids slowly closed, and the smile slowly left those lips.
Perhaps she was only sleeping for a little while.
Perhaps she would wake in a moment.
She had done that before at the church. He thought so. He wanted himself to believe so.
He waited. Longer.
Then longer still.
No more of the familiar warmth he had once carried on his back. No more of the lips she had once given him.
Nothing left for one like him.
" Please... Don't leave me... "
" My heart is lost... "
He rested his forehead against her head, both arms holding that woman close.
" I'm begging you... "
A single drop of blood fell onto her cheek, rolled down, then gently touched a carnation petal below.
" I... love you. Kristin. "
Perhaps it was too late for those words. Every heavy word that had been lodged in his throat for so long, yet now they felt lighter than ever.
Suddenly he heard a tiny sound from below.
" Kristin? "
He bent down and pressed his ear to her chest.
Thump-thump.
He pressed his ear deeper, but the sound did not grow louder. He realized it was not coming from her.
It was coming from him.
He reached up and touched his own chest.
His heart was still beating. So fast that nausea rose back up his throat. If only he could exchange this heart for hers. If only the thing inside him would stop. This damned curse he had been made to bear. He cursed his own undying body. Two fingers rose and pointed at his restless chest, and a sphere of blood appeared. A single shot and his chest would be blown away.
But then he remembered her wish. Every word seemed carved deep into his soul.
" I don't know how... I'm afraid I can't do it. "
He raised his hand to her hair, brushing it aside, as if trying to remember that face.
He gazed at the autumn before him.
 The most beautiful autumn she had ever given him.
